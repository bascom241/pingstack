# Globe texture

- File: `public/textures/earth-blue-marble.jpg`
- Dataset: NASA Blue Marble: Next Generation, January 2004 global base map.
- Source page: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
- Original asset: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/january/world.200401.3x5400x2700.jpg
- Credit: NASA Earth Observatory; Blue Marble: Next Generation produced by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.

The original texture is stored locally. The Three.js shader recolors its land and ocean in the rendered scene. The connection paths are decorative and do not represent carrier routes, live traffic, customer locations, or confirmed product coverage.
